🌟 About Mahurat

Mahurat (also spelled Muhurat or Muhurta) is an auspicious time period in Vedic astrology, considered ideal for beginning important activities, ceremonies, and endeavors. The concept is rooted in the ancient Vedic system of timekeeping and planetary influences.

Brahma Mahurat is considered the most sacred time of day, occurring approximately 96 minutes before sunrise. It is believed to be the best time for spiritual practices, meditation, and yoga.

Abhijit Mahurat is a highly auspicious period during the middle of the day, free from malefic influences. It is considered excellent for all types of activities.

Godhuli Mahurat occurs during twilight hours and is considered favorable for spiritual activities and worship.
